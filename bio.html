Originally from Chicago, IL, Dwelvan developed a love for music & dance at a young age performing at school, and in community, and church choirs and productions. In high school Dwelvan had the pleasure of traveling to and singing classical music in Italy as well as Germany, Austria and most of Eastern Europe. These experiences informed Dwelvan that performing was his destiny. Since earning his B.F.A. in Musical Theatre from Millikin University, Mr. David has garnered rave reviews for his portrayal of The Crab Man in the 1st National Tour of The Gershwins' Porgy and Bess, as well as his roles in Ain't Misbehavin', The Wiz, Caroline, or Change, Big River, Songs for a New World, & Cats. He originated the roles of Billy Bones & Job Anderson in Marc Robin's Treasure Island: A Musical Adventure as well as Thomas A. Dorsey in Bailiwick Chicago's Georgia Tom: The Father of Gospel Music. On-Camera, Dwelvan has been seen in episodes of Gotham, Law & Order SVU, and worked with Steve McQueen on his HBO project: Codes of Conduct. Nominations include Black Theater Alliance, Joseph Jefferson, Broadway World, & St. Louis Theatre Critics. Dwelvan is a proud member of Actors' Equity Association, SAG-AFTRA, and the American Guild of Musical Artists. He stands six-feet-four inches tall and hopes to serve as a source of inspiration for the next generation.
